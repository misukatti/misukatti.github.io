HammeritePropModel

Core: entities. Inherits HammeritePointEntityNode3D < Node3D

A model placed in a map (entity type prop_model): a crate, a statue, a barrel.

Its model is a name looked up in HammeriteModelLibrary; collision gives it a trimesh collider; it turns about all three axes and can be sized. It is drawn with the brush shader and lit by a lightmap of its own from the bake, at lightmap_scale units a texel. A model material may be dressed per prop through a TEXTURE_KEY_PREFIX property. HammeriteMap3D hides its mesh while no room it stands in is in view; its collider stays.

Constants

TEXTURE_KEY_PREFIX

const TEXTURE_KEY_PREFIX = "texture/"

What a prop names another texture for one of its model's materials under: texture/ and the material's name, lower-case and without an extension - texture/replace0 for a material called REPLACE0.GIF. A model made to be dressed differently wherever it stands - a door, a banner, a painting - has such a material, and each prop says what goes on it; the value is a texture's name, as a face's is.

Property descriptions

brush_material

var brush_material: ShaderMaterial = <Object>

The brush shader material each of the model's surfaces is drawn with a copy of. Copied when a surface's material is made, and those are shared between props, so changing it affects only materials made afterwards.
