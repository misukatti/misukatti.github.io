HammeriteAudioManager

Audio. Inherits RefCounted

One map's sound: plays sounds through its acoustic graph, sets the reverb, and tells listeners.

Manages all acoustic propagation logic, sound playback, and audio stream management. Stored in HammeriteMap3D.custom_data["acoustic_manager"] by the HammeriteAudio autoload, which makes a new one whenever the map or its variant changes; reach it with HammeriteAudio.get_manager(node) rather than holding on to one. Responsibilities: - Sound submission API for entities - AudioStreamPlayer3D pool management (dynamic allocation) - AudioStream caching with resource names as keys - All propagation calculations (zone detection, portal finding, path queries) - Position && volume updates each frame - Reverb application based on listener position - Multi-source sound support

Constants

MAX_POOL_SIZE

const MAX_POOL_SIZE = 64

Most AudioStreamPlayer3Ds one manager creates. Players are made as needed up to this; past it a sound that needs one is not played, and a warning says the pool is exhausted.

MOVEMENT_THRESHOLD

const MOVEMENT_THRESHOLD = 1.0

How far, in map units, a source or the listener must move before a sound is routed again.

MAX_CACHE_SIZE

const MAX_CACHE_SIZE = 256

Most routes the path cache holds; when full it is emptied rather than trimmed.

CACHE_GRID_SIZE

const CACHE_GRID_SIZE = 32.0

Grid, in map units, both ends of a route are snapped to for the path cache: two queries whose ends fall in the same pair of cells share one cached route.

UNFILTERED_ABOVE_HZ

const UNFILTERED_ABOVE_HZ = 12000.0

Above this a route has lost nothing worth filtering and the voice plays on the acoustic bus itself, rather than through a low-pass that would do nothing but cost.

EAR_AT_PORTAL_OCTAVES

const EAR_AT_PORTAL_OCTAVES = 1.0

How much brighter what comes through an opening is for an ear pressed to it: leaning in puts the ear AT the door rather than round the corner from it, and the corner is where the treble went. The loudness of it is the game's to say (press_ear_to_portal()); how a shut door sounds from an inch away is measurement, and is this.

MIN_PREDELAY_MSEC

const MIN_PREDELAY_MSEC = 20.0

The shortest predelay AudioEffectReverb documents. A room small enough to want less is one whose reverb its size has already turned off.

ENV_REFERENCE_DISTANCE

const ENV_REFERENCE_DISTANCE = 64.0

How weather indoors falls off with the way out to it: this far from the opening it is heard at full level, and it loses 6 dB for every doubling beyond. Measured from the listener along the path to the open air, so it is nothing in the doorway itself and grows the further in they go - which is what makes stepping inside a fade rather than a step. A doorway's width, in map units.

MAX_ENVIRONMENTAL_PORTALS

const MAX_ENVIRONMENTAL_PORTALS = 4

How many openings one environmental sound may be voiced through at once, the nearest way out among them. Each of the others costs a player, and past a handful they stop being distinguishable anyway.

SILENT_DB

const SILENT_DB = -80.0

Inaudible. Godot's own floor for a player's volume, and what a sound past its reach is set to rather than stopped: a looping stream stopped and started again clicks, and comes back at the top of the loop rather than where it would have been.

UNHEARD_LOSS_DB

const UNHEARD_LOSS_DB = 40.0

What a sound has lost when it is not worth a player: all that distance takes from one (_distance_falloff_db()), below which everything left is silence anyway.

ENV_MOST_DISTANCE_LOSS_DB

const ENV_MOST_DISTANCE_LOSS_DB = 40.0

And the most weather indoors loses to distance, however deep the building.

ENV_INDOOR_MUFFLE_DB

const ENV_INDOOR_MUFFLE_DB = 8.0

The muffling of being under a roof at all, reached this far in from the opening rather than at the threshold - a flat penalty on stepping inside was the sudden drop this is here to remove.

ENV_MUFFLE_DEPTH

const ENV_MUFFLE_DEPTH = 128.0

How far along the way out, in map units, ENV_INDOOR_MUFFLE_DB takes to be reached in full.

ENV_VOLUME_SLEW_DB

const ENV_VOLUME_SLEW_DB = 30.0

How fast the level follows where the listener is, in dB a second, and how fast the place it is heard from follows the opening it comes through, in units a second. Both only smooth; the target itself is continuous, so these are for the frame the nearest way out changes hands.

ENV_GLIDE_SPEED

const ENV_GLIDE_SPEED = 600.0

How fast, in map units a second, the place weather is heard from glides to a new opening.

ENV_PANNING_BLEND_SECONDS

const ENV_PANNING_BLEND_SECONDS = 0.5

How long going from all-round to one-sided takes, in seconds. Outdoors weather has no side and indoors it has the side of the way in, and switching on the frame the zone changed was heard as a jump in the ear turned away from the opening.

ENV_PANNING_DEPTH

const ENV_PANNING_DEPTH = 256.0

And how far from the open air it takes to come wholly from one side, measured along the way out rather than to the nearest doorway: at the opening itself the weather is right beside the listener and still mostly all round them, and it narrows as they walk further in. Measured to the doorway instead, stepping through an inner door into the next room widened it all round again.

HEARING_REFERENCE_DISTANCE

const HEARING_REFERENCE_DISTANCE = 64.0

Distance at which a sound is heard at its full source loudness, for the straight-line fallback below. Beyond it the usual inverse-square falloff applies.

MUFFLE_RUNGS_HZ

var MUFFLE_RUNGS_HZ: PackedFloat32Array = PackedFloat32Array(...)

The muffle ladder: one bus per rung, each a low-pass at that cutoff sending on into the acoustic bus, and a voice plays on the rung nearest what its route arrived with. A ladder rather than a filter per voice because a voice's own filter is dead here - see _voice_bus(). The rungs are roughly an octave apart, which is finer than the ear asks of a door, and one of them IS the water surface's cutoff: a voice heard across a pool's surface used to have a bus of its own and now takes that rung, at the same frequency it always had. A static var rather than a const only because one of the rungs is named rather than written: a const initialiser cannot read another script's const, and the water rung must BE the water's cutoff rather than a copy of it that drifts.

sound_types

var sound_types: HammeriteAudioSoundTypes = null

The game's sound-type table, pushed in by HammeriteAudioRuntime. Held here rather than read from a global so the manager is a self-contained object that can be built and tested on its own.

ambience

var ambience: HammeriteAudioAmbience = null

The bed of the room the listener is in.

Method descriptions

initialize()

func initialize(p_acoustic_graph: HammeriteAudioNodeGraph, p_map: HammeriteMap, p_map_3d: HammeriteMap3D) -> void

Initialize the manager with required references

create_positional_sound()

func create_positional_sound(properties: Dictionary[StringName, Variant]) -> int

Create a handle for a positional sound, or -1 when sound_file does not resolve in HammeriteAudioRuntime.sounds. Nothing plays until play_sound(). Keys read from properties: sound_file, position (world space), volume (dB, default 0), loop (default true), use_acoustic_propagation (default true), max_distance (0 is no limit), attenuation_scale (default 1) and free_when_finished (default false). Misspelled keys are ignored without a warning. The handle stays valid until delete_sound().

create_environmental_sound()

func create_environmental_sound(properties: Dictionary) -> int

Create a handle for an environmental sound (rain, wind), or -1 when no player is free or sound_file does not resolve. Holds a pooled player from now until delete_sound(). Keys read from properties: sound_file, position, volume (dB, default 0) and loop (default true); direction is set afterwards through update_sound_property().

play_sound()

func play_sound(handle: int) -> void

Start the sound behind handle, placed and levelled for the listener before it starts. Pushes an error for an unknown handle. A non-looping positional sound already out of earshot is not played at all, so is_sound_playing() stays false for it.

is_sound_playing()

func is_sound_playing(handle: int) -> bool

Is the sound behind handle currently playing?

stop_sound()

func stop_sound(handle: int) -> void

Stop a playing sound. The handle stays valid and can be played again; an unknown handle is ignored.

delete_sound()

func delete_sound(handle: int) -> void

Delete a sound and remove its handle This releases the audio player back to the pool and removes the handle from active sounds

update_sound_property()

func update_sound_property(handle: int, property: StringName, value) -> bool

Update a sound property Supported properties: volume, position, loop, attenuation_scale, use_acoustic_propagation, direction. Returns false, with an error or a warning, for an unknown handle or property, a value of the wrong type, or a property the sound's kind does not have (position on an environmental sound, direction on a positional one).

press_ear_to_portal()

func press_ear_to_portal(portal_name: String, gain_db: float) -> void

Press the ear this manager plays for to the opening named portal_name: every sound that comes through it plays gain_db louder, and nothing else changes. Listening at a door. This is what is PLAYED. What a HammeriteAudioListener3D hears is its own HammeriteAudioListener3D.ear_to_portal, so a player's captions can follow their ear.

take_ear_from_portal()

func take_ear_from_portal() -> void

Undo press_ear_to_portal(): nothing is heard louder through any opening.

update()

func update(delta: float) -> void

Update all active sounds for the current camera's listener, and release finished one-shots. The HammeriteMap3D holding this manager calls it every frame; calling it as well advances everything twice.

update_for_listener()

func update_for_listener(delta: float, listener_position: Vector3) -> void

Update every active sound for a listener at listener_position. Split out of update() so the listener can come from somewhere other than the current camera - a test with no viewport, a second listener in split screen - rather than being reachable only through whatever camera happens to be current. A listener_position of exactly Vector3.ZERO is taken as no listener at all, and nothing is updated.

clear_cache()

func clear_cache() -> void

Clear path cache

register_listener()

func register_listener(listener: HammeriteAudioListener3D) -> void

Register a listener under each of the sound types it subscribes to. A HammeriteAudioListener3D does this itself on entering the tree; the types are read now, so a later change needs a re-register.

unregister_listener()

func unregister_listener(listener: HammeriteAudioListener3D) -> void

Remove a listener from every type it was registered under.

report_sound()

func report_sound(type: StringName, position: Vector3, emitter: Node = null) -> void

Report a sound of type made at position, and tell whoever could hear it. The type is checked first and cheaply: with nothing subscribed there is no work to do, which is what makes it affordable to call this on every footstep. Then, per listener, straight-line distance against the type's range - a cheap reject that avoids pathfinding for sounds that could not possibly carry - and only then the propagation query, which is the expensive part. emitter is whatever made the noise, used so a listener can disregard its own.

cleanup()

func cleanup() -> void

Stop and delete every sound and free the pooled players. Called when the manager is replaced or its map is cleared; every handle it gave out is invalid afterwards.

class AcousticEnvironmentalSoundData

Environmental sound data (weather, ambient, etc)

One player for the whole sound, indoors and out, and only ever moved and levelled - never handed over to another. A second player is a second copy of the loop starting from its beginning, and that is heard as a jump however well the two volumes are matched.

Whether heard_from has been placed yet. The first placement is a jump, not a glide.

other_ways_out

var other_ways_out: Dictionary[int, AudioStreamPlayer3D] = {}

One player per further opening the weather also comes in through, by portal node id. The nearest way out is not among them - that one is AcousticSoundData.player, which has been running the loop since the listener was outdoors and is never handed over.

other_volume_db

var other_volume_db: Dictionary[int, float] = {}

What each of those is at now, so one fades in and out on its own as the listener moves.
