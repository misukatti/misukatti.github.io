Carved, not built

Solid is the default. Air brushes carve rooms out of it, and the compiler works out the rest: which air is one room, where rooms open into each other, which walls are visible. Overlap to extend a space, abut to divide one.

Lighting, acoustics and navigation are all read off that one compiled world, so a room is lit, heard and walked the moment it is carved.

An editor inside your game

The map editor runs in the game itself, with its own manual under Help. Ship it, and your players can make maps too.

GPU lightmapper

Bakes bounced light for every surface on the GPU, from the editor or from a script in a build pipeline.

Sound that follows the rooms

Sound travels through the openings between rooms rather than through walls, and AI can hear it the same way.

Navigation from the same world

Walkable ground and traversal links come straight from the carved rooms, as Godot navigation meshes.
