HammeriteAudioBrushProperties

Audio. Inherits RefCounted

The acoustic character of a room, authored on the air brush that carves it.

In the subtractive world an air brush IS a room and the compiler already derives which rooms exist and which openings join them. What it cannot derive is intent: whether a courtyard should be treated as open sky, how much weather gets into a covered alley, whether a chamber should ring like stone or be deadened like a carpeted study. Those are authoring decisions, and before this schema existed there was nowhere on a brush to put them - which is why acoustics needed a parallel world of hand-placed zone entities to hold them.

So: hammerite-audio declares them, core stores them sparsely on the brush, and the audio graph reads them off the cells. Core never learns what a reverb preset is.

Every property AUTO-derives by default, so an author who places nothing and touches nothing still gets a working graph - the overrides exist for the cases derivation gets wrong.

Schema id, and therefore the namespace of every key below.

Native since 2.0 (#269): SCHEMA_ID and the KEY_ constants are static methods of the same names in lower case; PRESET_AUTO, an integer, stays.
